Detection engines
01
Spatial duplicate detection

Computes great-circle distance between every pair of same-category works within a constituency. Two assets of the same type sanctioned within the radius threshold are flagged as a probable duplicate claim.

Haversine formulathreshold ≤ 100 mSQL + Python
02
Collusive bidding detection

Builds a bipartite graph of contractors and tenders. Edge weight counts how often two contractors appear in the same bidding pool; repeated co-bidding with rotating winners indicates cover bidding.

NetworkX graphweight ≥ 2 tenderspairwise edges
03
Rate benchmark & cost outlier analysis

Compares each sanctioned amount against the state PWD Schedule of Rates ceiling for its category, then applies a standard-score test to surface costs that deviate sharply from the population.

PWD SoR ceilingratio ≥ 1.5×Z-score > 2.5
04
Fund stagnation monitoring

Measures elapsed time since sanction against reported physical progress, and tracks constituency-level unspent balances to identify capital that has been released but not deployed.

SQL temporal query≥ 180 days< 20% progress
Why rule-based, not a neural model

Every alert this system raises must survive scrutiny in a departmental inquiry. A finding an officer cannot independently recompute cannot be acted upon.

Each detection therefore carries its own arithmetic — the measured distance, the rate ratio, the elapsed days, the co-bid count — recorded alongside the alert and reproducible from the source records.

Stated limitations

Advisory tier is low-specificity. The demo dataset assigns every constituency an identical unspent ratio, so the unspent-balance rule fires almost universally. It is reported separately from confirmed findings and excluded from headline counts.

Cartel detection reports pairs. A colluding group of three currently surfaces as three pairwise relationships rather than one ring. Clique consolidation is not yet implemented.

Project data is demonstration scale. Constituency and allocation records are the official published dataset; the sanctioned works used to exercise the detectors are synthetic.
